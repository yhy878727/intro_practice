A little about me

Understand the field.
Then build the bridge.

I started my career as a nurse in the operating room. I later moved to medical AI services, where my work included onboarding users, answering their questions, and supporting service operations. These roles showed me the practical problems that come up when healthcare teams start using new technology.

I am currently a graduate student in Health Data Science, learning how to work with data, compare methods, and evaluate results. My research uses multimodal models to read values from medical device screens. Through this work, I keep asking how a tool can meet users’ needs and get better through use in real settings.
